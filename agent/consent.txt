TALLY SYNC AGENT - WHAT THIS PROGRAM DOES

This program runs in the background on this computer and talks to Tally ERP 9 or TallyPrime
on this computer (or the Tally PC you configure).

After you approve it on the Tally Sync website, it READS:
  - ledgers (names, groups, balances)
  - stock items (names, units, quantity and value)
  - vouchers (sales, purchases, receipts, payments and their entries)
from ONLY the Tally companies you tick on your dashboard.

It does not change or delete anything in Tally.
Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to your Tally Sync account.
You can pause it from the tray icon, or revoke it from the website at any time.
Uninstalling removes the program; you can also ask us to delete your synced data.
